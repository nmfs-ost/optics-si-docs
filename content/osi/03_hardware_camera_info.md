# Hardware and Camera Information

## Camera classes
- Action cameras and survey cameras
- Fixed underwater observatory cameras
- Towed and ROV platform cameras
- Vessel-deployed still and video systems

## Minimum capture metadata
- Camera make and model
- Lens/focal details
- Frame rate and resolution
- Time zone and timestamp policy
- Platform and mission identifiers
- Environmental context (depth, turbidity, lighting)

## Field capture standards
- Verify clock synchronization before deployment.
- Capture calibration frames when available.
- Record lighting setup and changes during collection.
- Preserve raw source files before transcoding.

## Quality checklist
- Focus and exposure checks
- Motion blur review
- Lens obstruction and glare review
- Corrupt file scan and checksum validation
